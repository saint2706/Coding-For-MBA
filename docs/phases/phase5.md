# Phase 5 • Business Intelligence

Phase 5 turns technical confidence into enterprise BI strategy. Learners translate analytics into
stakeholder impact, govern reliable data pipelines, and design dashboards that land with executives.

## What you will practice

- Defining BI operating models, stakeholder maps, and success metrics.
- Building governed data foundations with repeatable ingestion and modeling patterns.
- Translating analytics to decision support through dashboards, experimentation, and storytelling.
- Crafting the career assets and narratives that move you into BI leadership roles.
