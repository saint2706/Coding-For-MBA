# Day 89 – Capstone Project - Part 2

## Introduction

In the second part of the capstone project, you will focus on data analysis, visualization, and presenting your findings.

## Part 2: Analysis and Presentation

*   **Data Analysis:** Perform in-depth analysis to answer the key business questions.
*   **Data Visualization:** Create compelling visualizations to communicate your insights.
*   **Final Presentation:** Prepare and deliver a presentation of your findings to a non-technical audience.

## Exercises

1.  Create a Jupyter Notebook with your data analysis and visualizations.
2.  Develop a slide deck for your final presentation.
3.  Record a short video of you presenting your findings.
