## Introduction

This lesson focuses on the critical importance of data governance and security in a BI environment.
We will cover the key principles of data governance, common security threats, and best practices for
protecting data.

## Data Governance Frameworks

A data governance framework provides a structure for managing an organization's data assets. It
defines roles, responsibilities, and processes to ensure data is handled consistently and in line
with business objectives.

### Popular Frameworks

- **DAMA-DMBOK (Data Management Body of Knowledge):** A comprehensive framework that covers 11 key
  knowledge areas of data management, with data governance at its core.
- **COBIT (Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies):** A framework that aligns
  IT and data policies with business objectives, with a strong focus on risk management and control
  monitoring.
- **DCAM (Data Management Capability Assessment Model):** A framework that helps organizations
  assess their data management capabilities and provides a roadmap for improvement.

## Data Quality Management

Data quality management is a set of practices that aim to maintain a high level of data quality.
High-quality data is accurate, complete, consistent, timely, and relevant.

### Best Practices

1. **Establish a Data Governance Framework:** A solid framework is the foundation for data quality.
1. **Conduct Regular Data Audits:** Periodically check your data for inaccuracies, inconsistencies,
   and duplicates.
1. **Implement Validation Rules:** Use automated checks to validate data at the point of entry.
1. **Standardize Data:** Ensure that data is in a consistent format across the organization.
1. **Clean and Maintain Data:** Regularly cleanse your data to remove errors and outdated
   information.

## Data Privacy and Compliance

Data privacy and compliance are about protecting individuals' rights and adhering to legal and
regulatory requirements for data handling.

### Key Regulations

- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** A regulation in EU law on data protection and
  privacy for all individuals within the European Union and the European Economic Area.
- **CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act):** A state statute intended to enhance privacy rights and
  consumer protection for residents of California.
- **HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act):** A US federal law that required
  the creation of national standards to protect sensitive patient health information from being
  disclosed without the patient's consent or knowledge.

### Best Practices

1. **Develop a Comprehensive Data Privacy Framework:** Establish clear policies and controls for
   data handling.
1. **Conduct Regular Risk Assessments:** Identify and mitigate security vulnerabilities.
1. **Implement Data Minimization Strategies:** Collect and store only the data that is absolutely
   necessary.
1. **Ensure Transparency in Data Processing:** Be clear with individuals about how their data is
   being used.
1. **Establish Clear Data Access Controls:** Restrict access to sensitive data to authorized
   personnel.
