# Day 90 – Career Workshop and Next Steps

## Introduction

This final lesson is a career workshop designed to help you leverage your new skills in the job market. We will cover resume building, interview preparation, and strategies for continued learning.

## Topics Covered

*   **Building a Data-Driven Resume:** Learn how to showcase your projects and skills on your resume.
*   **Technical Interviewing:** Prepare for technical interviews and whiteboarding challenges.
*   **Networking and Personal Branding:** Develop your personal brand and network with industry professionals.
*   **Continuing Education:** Explore resources for staying up-to-date with the latest trends in BI and data science.

## Exercises

1.  Update your resume to include your capstone project.
2.  Practice answering common technical interview questions.
3.  Create a 30-60-90 day plan for your continued learning.
