Why Python?

Pro Tip

Indentation is mandatory in Python. Make sure you use 4 spaces per indentation level to avoid IndentationError.

Variables & Data Types

In Python, you don't need to declare variable types explicitly. The interpreter infers them for you. This is called dynamic typing.
