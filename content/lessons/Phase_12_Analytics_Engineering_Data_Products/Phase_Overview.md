# 📊 Phase 12: Analytics Engineering & Data Products

> *"Phase 11 taught you to build the engine. Phase 12 teaches you to drive it — turning infrastructure into insights, metrics into decisions, and data into products that generate revenue."*

---

## Phase Overview

Phase 12 is where technology meets business. You've built the infrastructure (Phase 11) — now you learn to create value from it. This phase covers the roles, tools, and strategies that turn raw data into trusted analytics and revenue-generating data products.

**This phase answers:** "How do I go from 'we have a data warehouse' to 'every team makes better decisions with data and we've launched a data product that generates $1M ARR'?"

---

## What You'll Build

By the end of Phase 12, you'll have:

- 🎯 Defined the **analytics engineer role** and designed a data team structure
- 📐 Built a **semantic layer** with unified metric definitions (dbt Semantic Layer / Cube.js)
- 🔓 Designed a **self-serve analytics** framework with guardrails
- 🕸️ Evaluated **data mesh** principles for your organization
- 📊 Mastered **product analytics** — retention cohorts, funnels, engagement metrics
- 🧪 Designed rigorous **A/B tests** with proper statistical methodology
- 💎 Created a **data product** with a business case and go-to-market plan
- 🏆 Pitched a **complete data product** as your curriculum capstone
